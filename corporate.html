Why enterprise AI stalls

Most organizations don't lack ideas or models. They get stuck in the same four places.

Pilots that never reach production

Demos impress, then stall on architecture, evaluation, security and operations nobody planned for.

Teams skilled in demos, not production

Engineers can call a model, but not build reliable RAG, agents, guardrails and monitoring around it.

No clear way to pick the first use case

Long idea lists with no shared view of value, feasibility, data readiness or risk.

Governance and cost surprises, late

Compliance, security reviews and runaway inference costs appear after the budget is spent.

We close each of these gaps, one engagement at a time.

Questions enterprises ask

Can you work under an NDA and keep our data confidential?

Yes. We can sign an NDA before any discussion of your data. Capstones and clinics can use synthetic or anonymised data if production data can't be shared.

Do you deliver onsite?

Yes. Level Up cohorts and AI Clinics can run online, onsite or hybrid. Travel for onsite delivery is included in the proposal.

How large can a Level Up cohort be?

We size cohorts to your team, from a single squad to several parallel cohorts. Tell us the number of people and their roles, and we'll recommend a format.

Can the programs be customised?

Yes. We adapt examples, the capstone and the emphasis of modules to your domain, technology stack and goals.

How is pricing worked out?

Every engagement gets a tailored proposal based on scope, team size, format and duration. There are no hidden fees.

Do you invoice with GST, and can international teams pay in USD?

Indian organizations are invoiced in INR with GST. International organizations can be invoiced in USD.

Can you support teams in other time zones?

Yes. We regularly schedule sessions that work across IST and US Eastern Time, and can plan for other regions.

Which programs are available now?

FDE 100X™ and Multi-Agent Systems Engineering are available now. The other eight programs are coming soon and can be pre-booked for enterprise cohorts.
